Put it on your home screen (once)
  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

After that, tap the Tube Player icon like any other app.

Tap the full-screen button in the player and turn the phone sideways for a big picture.
