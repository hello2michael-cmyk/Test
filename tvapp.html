The TV app shows an address like 192.168.1.23:8080. Type it here once; it is remembered.

Phone and TV must be on the same Wi‑Fi. Or just scan the QR code the TV shows.

One-time install on the TCL TV

  1. On the TV, open the Google Play Store and install Downloader (by AFTVnews).
  2. TV Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources: allow Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader, type this address and press Go:
    …
  4. When it downloads, choose Install, then Open.
  5. In TV Screen Share, press Start sharing, then Start now on the system question. Allow sound if asked.
  6. The TV shows an address and a QR code. Scan it with this phone, or type the address above.

Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+ protect their picture, so they show as black. YouTube, games, menus and most apps work.
