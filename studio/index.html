Footage (generated video)

Real text-to-video, free: each scene is generated by LTX-Video on Hugging Face's shared GPUs (about 1–3 minutes per clip, queue permitting). The free GPU quota is per day; when it runs out the remaining scenes use the sources below automatically. For unlimited free generation run the Colab notebook in the repository (colab/ folder) and paste its gradio.live URL above.

Stock clips are real filmed people and places, free for YouTube under the Pexels and Pixabay licences. Without any key, scenes get a photoreal AI image with a slow pan and zoom. Scenes with neither fall back to motion graphics; credits are added to youtube.txt.

Get a key at fal.ai → Dashboard → Keys. Clips are generated in parallel in the cloud and composited with titles, captions and the voice on this phone. A six-scene video costs roughly 6 × the per-clip price shown. Scenes whose clip fails fall back to motion graphics automatically.

Music

Use music you have the rights to (for example the YouTube Audio Library). The mixer ducks it automatically while the voice speaks.

About

Everything runs in this browser: the language model (WebLLM / llama.cpp), the voice (Kokoro), the renderer (Canvas) and the encoder (WebCodecs). Nothing is uploaded. Source: github.com/hello2michael-cmyk/Test
