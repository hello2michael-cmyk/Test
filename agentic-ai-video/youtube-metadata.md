An AI agent perceives its environment, reasons about a goal, takes actions with tools, and checks the result in a loop until the job is done. Agentic AI is a system where one or more agents work autonomously toward a bigger goal, splitting it into tasks, using tools, and coordinating with each other, with only light human supervision.
